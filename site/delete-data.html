Delete your Lauk data

Lauk (Google Play: dev.edycu.lauk) has no accounts, so a deletion request is one email. This page explains how to ask, what is deleted, and what is kept.

How to request deletion

  1. Email edy.cu@live.com with the subject “Delete my Lauk data”.
  2. If you bought anything in Lauk, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt email, or at play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). This is how we find your record.
  3. If you never bought anything, RevenueCat holds only a random id and that one attribute. It is not linked to your name, email or device, so we cannot find it from anything you could send us, and it identifies no one.
  4. We delete the data listed below within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your RevenueCat customer record: the anonymous app user id, your purchase history as RevenueCat stores it, and the one eating_context attribute (e.g. student).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play order records (what was bought, when, and the amount) are kept by Google under Google’s own retention rules, which we do not control.
  • Where tax or accounting rules require it, a minimal purchase record (product, date, amount — no personal details) may be retained for the legally required period, then deleted.
  • Deletion does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data on your phone

Your plate checks, the “how was it” log and your settings live only in the app’s private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Lauk, or Android Settings → Apps → Lauk → Storage → Clear storage, deletes them immediately. We never receive them.
